# SmartSave — Product Requirements

## 1. Product Name

SmartSave

## 2. Product Type

Goal-Based Personal Finance & Financial Wellness Platform

## 3. Problem Statement

People often struggle to convert their income and spending information
into realistic, measurable financial goals and savings plans.

SmartSave analyzes financial behaviour and provides personalized
goal plans, financial health insights and recommendations to help
users improve their financial wellness.

## 4. Target Users

- Young working professionals
- Students
- Individuals who want to improve their saving habits
- People planning specific financial goals

## 5. Core Purpose

SmartSave helps users:

- Understand their income and expenses
- Create financial goals
- Calculate required savings
- Track goal progress
- Assess financial health
- Identify potential spending improvements
- Receive personalized recommendations

## 6. Example Goal

A user wants to purchase a bike.

Income: ₹60,000/month
Expenses: ₹40,000/month
Current Savings: ₹50,000
Goal Amount: ₹1,50,000
Time: 12 months

SmartSave calculates the required monthly saving,
remaining amount and goal feasibility.
